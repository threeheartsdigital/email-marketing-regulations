# China
China's email-specific rules are set out in the Measures for the Administration of Internet Email Services (2006), often referred to as the Regulations of Email Services (RES). Since November 2021, the Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL) also applies: email addresses are personal information, so marketing use of them must meet the PIPL's consent requirements. The Network Data Security Management Regulations, in force since January 2025, further tighten the PIPL's consent rules – consent cannot be obtained in a misleading or coercive way, and collection must be limited to what is necessary.

## Required content
The RES requires email marketers to make sure recipients are aware they are receiving promotional content by putting "AD" (or the Chinese equivalent, 广告) in their subject lines. Email content must not violate the Telecommunications Regulations of the People's Republic of China. In general, this means that politically sensitive or mature content is prohibited. This includes advertisements for pornography, firearms, gambling, tobacco and illegal drugs. Senders must not conceal or forge sender information, and must provide a valid contact method for unsubscribing, kept valid for at least 30 days.

## Consent
The RES requires prior express consent before marketing emails can be sent, however the opt-in methods are not specified. The PIPL adds that consent must be informed, voluntary and withdrawable, with an easy way to withdraw it.

## Notable exceptions or requirements
Recipients must be able to opt-out from receiving emails, and opt-outs must be honoured.

## Penalties
Violations of the RES are subject to fines of up to 10,000 CNY, rising to up to 30,000 CNY where there are illegal gains. The penalties are enforced through a report-based system, where consumers file an official complaint about illegal email activity.

The PIPL carries far heavier penalties: up to 1 million CNY for ordinary violations, and up to 50 million CNY or 5% of the previous year's annual turnover for grave violations, alongside personal fines for responsible individuals.
